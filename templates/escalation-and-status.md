# Escalation and Status — Template

**Version:** 1.1.0 | **Licence:** CC BY 4.0

Set tolerances when a mandate is approved, escalate on *forecast* breach rather than after failure, and report status in a way that surfaces decisions instead of hiding them.

**Purpose:** Make exceptions, forecast breaches, decisions and delivery confidence visible to the authority that can act.

**When to use:** At initiative mandate approval, during routine governance reviews and whenever a tolerance breach is forecast.

**How to use:** Agree tolerances in advance, report actuals separately from forecasts, identify the decision required and owner, and maintain a RAID log with actions and due dates.

Related: [Executive Playbook](../frameworks/strategy-execution-governance-playbook.md), Section 5, and [Digital Portfolio Governance](../frameworks/digital-portfolio-governance.md), Sections 7 and 8.

## Status report

Report these separately. A traffic-light colour may summarise a defined rule but never replaces the explanation.

| Element | Content |
|---|---|
| Actual progress | What has been completed |
| Forecast | What is expected to happen, and when |
| Confidence | How reliable the forecast is, and why |
| Top risk / issue | What could materially change the outcome |
| Decision needed | The management action required, from whom, by when |
| Outcome evidence | Adoption and outcome data, not only delivery output |

Colour rule: **green** means within agreed tolerances *with evidence*; it does not mean "no issues". Do not report green where material risks are merely being deferred.

## RAID log (columns)

| ID | Type (risk / assumption / issue / decision) | Description | Owner | Next action | Due | Status | Linked initiative or dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Every high-priority item needs an owner and a next action; overdue items need an explicit disposition.
