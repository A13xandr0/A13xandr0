# KPI Dictionary — Template

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Licence:** CC BY 4.0

| KPI | Definition / calculation | Unit | Frequency | Accountable owner | Decision use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Outcome attainment | Actual outcome compared with approved target | % / defined scale | Monthly / quarterly | Outcome owner | Continue, change or stop |
| Milestone predictability | Milestones achieved on/before forecast ÷ milestones due | % | Monthly | Initiative lead | Recovery and confidence |
| Forecast variance | (Current estimate − approved baseline) ÷ baseline | % | Monthly | Finance / initiative lead | Escalate cost pressure |
| Critical dependency health | Critical dependencies with owner and response ÷ total critical dependencies | % | Monthly | Initiative lead | Resolve constraints |
| Adoption | Eligible users or transactions using capability as intended ÷ eligible population | % | Monthly | Service owner | Adoption action |
| Benefit realisation | Verified benefit compared with time-phased plan | % / value | Quarterly | Benefit owner | Validate value |
| Risk-action timeliness | Risk actions completed by due date ÷ actions due | % | Monthly | Risk owner | Escalate exposure |
| Decision lead time | Median working days from complete request to decision | Days | Monthly | Governance owner | Remove bottlenecks |
