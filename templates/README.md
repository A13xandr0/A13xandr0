# Executive Governance Templates

Reusable working artefacts supporting the frameworks in this repository. Adapt them to context; they are not universal prescriptions.

## How to use the templates

Use only the artefacts needed for the decision and its level of materiality. The typical sequence is:

1. **Frame:** describe the problem, users, intended outcome and baseline in the [Initiative Canvas](initiative-canvas.md).
2. **Assess:** identify mandatory obligations separately, then compare genuine discretionary options using the [Weighted Scoring Matrix](weighted-scoring-matrix.md).
3. **Decide:** document the rationale, authority, conditions and review trigger in the [Decision Record](decision-record.md).
4. **Mobilise and govern:** clarify accountability with the [RACI Matrix](raci-matrix.md), establish decision forums where needed, and set tolerances and escalation routes.
5. **Measure and learn:** define measures and benefit ownership, monitor adoption and outcomes, and use evidence to continue, change or stop.

This is a reference sequence, not a compulsory stage-gate process. The Executive Playbook explains how to adapt it.

## Common documentation convention

Each template is structured around the practical questions relevant to its purpose. Where applicable, its opening guidance should make clear:

- **Purpose:** what the artefact is intended to support;
- **When to use:** the decision, lifecycle point or trigger for using it;
- **How to use:** the steps or completion guidance;
- **Related materials:** the relevant framework and companion artefacts;
- **Example:** an illustrative completed entry or worked example where this materially improves understanding.

Not every artefact needs identical headings. A checklist, register, scorecard and decision worksheet should retain the structure that makes it usable. The common convention is to provide consistent orientation without forcing unlike tools into the same format.
