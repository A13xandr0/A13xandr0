# RACI Matrix — Template

**Version:** 1.1.0 | **Licence:** CC BY 4.0

A RACI matrix is a responsibility-assignment template that clarifies who is Accountable, Responsible, Consulted and Informed for governance activities and decisions. It supports clear decision rights but does not replace named ownership.

**Purpose:** Make participation and accountability explicit for recurring activities and material decisions.

**When to use:** When establishing or revising a governance operating model, initiative mandate or cross-functional process.

**How to use:** Adapt role labels to delegated authority, assign one accountable role per row, identify responsible contributors and document any specialist decision rights separately.

| Activity / decision | Executive sponsor | Portfolio authority | Initiative lead | Service owner | Architecture / assurance | Benefit owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Confirm strategic outcome | A | C | R | C | C | C |
| Approve initiative mandate | A | R | C | C | C | C |
| Allocate portfolio capacity | C | A | C | C | C | I |
| Define solution and controls | I | I | R | C | A* | I |
| Manage delivery within tolerance | I | I | A/R | C | C | I |
| Accept service transition | I | I | R | A | C | C |
| Validate realised benefit | I | I | C | R | I | A |
| Decide continuation or scale | A | R | C | C | C | C |

*The designated design or assurance authority is accountable for its specialist decision within delegated remit; the executive sponsor remains accountable for the overall investment and outcome.
