# Decision Record — Fictional Worked Example

**Version:** 1.1.0 | **Licence:** CC BY 4.0

> Fictional illustration only. It does not describe an actual organisation, project, decision, result or personal achievement.

- **Decision ID:** EX-SVC-001
- **Title:** Select a bounded service-improvement pilot before platform investment
- **Status:** Illustrative — pilot approved in the fictional scenario
- **Decision owner:** Executive sponsor (fictional role)
- **Review trigger:** Pilot evidence review or material change in service risk / assumptions

## Context and outcome

A fictional organisation experiences slow resolution of internal service requests. The desired outcome is improved resolution time and user experience while maintaining service quality and avoiding premature technology commitment. A reliable quantified baseline has not yet been validated; establishing it is part of the pilot.

## Options considered

1. Process clarification and ownership pilot.
2. Targeted workflow tooling.
3. Broad platform replacement.
4. Defer / do nothing.

## Evidence and assumptions

The illustrative scoring matrix rates the options 4.60/5, 3.45/5 and 2.10/5 respectively. Ratings are assumed, not measured. Assumptions to validate: process ownership and hand-offs contribute materially to delay; the pilot can be bounded safely; service data can establish a baseline; users and operational owners can participate; no mandatory obligation requires immediate platform replacement.

## Decision and rationale

**Proceed with a bounded process clarification pilot. Do not approve broad platform replacement at this stage.** Retain targeted workflow tooling as a conditional option if evidence identifies a workflow constraint that process clarification cannot resolve.

The pilot is the most reversible option and offers the fastest route to useful evidence under the fictional assumptions. It reduces uncertainty before a larger, less reversible investment.

## Conditions

- Validate baseline, scope and measurement method before intervention.
- Name service owner, Initiative lead and benefit owner.
- Agree service-quality safeguards and escalation thresholds.
- Record user feedback and operational exceptions.
- Do not scale based solely on activity or anecdotal satisfaction.

## Actions

| Action | Owner (role) | Due / trigger | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Validate baseline and data quality | Service owner | Before pilot | Approved baseline definition |
| Confirm scope and safeguards | Sponsor + service owner | Before pilot | Pilot mandate |
| Run pilot and report exceptions | Initiative lead | During pilot | Progress / issue log |
| Assess adoption and outcome | Benefit owner | Review point | KPI and user evidence |
| Recommend continue, adapt or stop | Sponsor | Evidence review | Updated decision record |

## Risks and unresolved questions

Baseline data may be incomplete; delays may be driven by factors outside scope; process changes may shift rather than reduce workload; adoption may be weak; mandatory technical or contractual constraints may emerge. Record dissent and specialist assurance issues before treating a decision as final.

## Review outcome

Compare actual results with the validated baseline and agreed target trajectory. Decide to continue, adapt, add targeted tooling, scale or stop. Record evidence and rationale in a new decision entry.
