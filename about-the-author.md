# Alexandro Sances

![Alexandro Sances — official illustrated portrait](assets/alexandro-sances-portrait.jpg){ align=right width="240" }

**Technology Strategy, Execution & Governance | Digital Transformation | Technology Leadership**

Alexandro Sances is a technology and digital-transformation executive with more than 30 years of professional experience, including over 17 years in international and multicultural environments. His work has spanned telecommunications and satellite communications, insurtech, connected mobility, telematics, IoT, data-driven services and digital platforms.

His experience combines executive leadership with advisory, board and mentoring activities. Across these environments, his responsibilities and areas of work have included technology strategy, digital transformation, IT and operations, product and service platforms, data-driven business models, organisational execution and technology governance.

His professional background includes international technology businesses and ventures, as well as work in Italy's public-sector digital transformation. Since 2022, he has contributed to the Department for Digital Transformation of the Presidency of the Council of Ministers, with responsibilities involving PMO and internal information systems.

## Professional focus

His current professional interests include:

- **Technology strategy and execution:** connecting strategic outcomes, capabilities, technology choices, investment and measurable delivery.
- **Digital and technology governance:** clarifying decision rights, accountability, portfolio priorities, dependencies and benefits.
- **Enterprise AI and RAG:** governing information readiness, provenance, access, evaluation, security and lifecycle risk.
- **Open-source strategy:** assessing ecosystem health, supply-chain dependencies, stewardship, contribution and exit readiness.
- **Digital infrastructure and sustainability:** connecting service demand with capacity, energy, water, resilience and lifecycle economics.
- **Digital transformation and operating models:** translating strategic intent into organisational capabilities and sustainable execution.

## Frameworks and publications

The [Technology Strategy, Execution & Governance repository](https://github.com/A13xandr0/A13xandr0) is an independently authored collection of reference frameworks, executive playbooks and reusable governance templates.

The material reflects a consistent approach: start from outcomes and service needs; make capabilities, options and dependencies visible; establish decision authority and accountability; govern execution through evidence; and assess adoption and realised benefits.

The repository is maintained on GitHub as the canonical source. The [published documentation website](https://a13xandr0.github.io/A13xandr0/) provides a navigable presentation of the same material.
